✦ Written in Destiny · United in Faith · Bound in Love ✦

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Every beautiful union begins not by coincidence, but by divine design.

In Islamic tradition, marriage is considered one of the most sacred covenants—a spiritual partnership where two souls unite with the shared goal of seeking Allah's pleasure, inspiring one another toward righteousness, and cultivating a haven of peace and tranquility.

Our story is a testament to the beauty of Allah's timing and the blessing of sincere prayer. What began as a meeting of hearts and shared values gradually blossomed into an unshakeable bond—anchored in mutual respect, patience (Sabr), and profound gratitude (Shukr).

A Nikah is not merely the union of two individuals; it is the joyous coming together of two loving families. With the constant guidance, warmth, and heartfelt Duas of our cherished parents—Mr. Ameerudheen & Mrs. Shahanabi and Mr. Ashraf & Mrs. Shameera—our households have united in joy, celebration, and gratitude to the Almighty.

As the beloved Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) taught us: “There is nothing like marriage for two who love one another.” (Sunan Ibn Majah). On December 27, 2026, we prepare to solemnize our Nikah, making an oath before Allah (SWT) and our loved ones to be each other's garment of solace, comfort, and steadfast partner throughout all seasons of life.

“
بَارَكَ اللَّهُ لَكُمَا وَبَارَكَ عَلَيْكُمَا وَجَمَعَ بَيْنَكُمَا فِي خَيْرٍ

“May Allah bless you, and shower His blessings upon you, and unite you both in goodness, love, and piety.” — Ameen 🤲

With faith in our hearts, gratitude on our lips, and the Duas of our families—our sacred journey begins.

Surrounded by your love and prayers, we eagerly look forward to sharing this momentous day of celebration with you.

On December 27, 2026, we say “Qubool Hai.”
And forever in the shade of Allah's mercy starts here. ❤️
